Using Return on Uni

Return on Uni is free to use. The database is made up of answers shared by graduates. It is general information, not career, financial or study advice, and individual outcomes vary.

Sharing your story

Please answer honestly and only about yourself. Don't include names, employers or anything that could identify you or someone else. We may remove answers that look false, duplicated or abusive.

Independence

Universities can't pay to appear, rank higher or change results.

Liability

We work to keep the database accurate, but we can't guarantee any figure. Use it as one input alongside other research when choosing a course.
