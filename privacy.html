Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Your account. You sign in with Google or with a link we email you. We keep your email address, your name and profile picture if you sign in with Google, when you joined, and whether you've shared your story. There are no passwords: we never see or store one.

Your answers. When you fill in the Share form we collect your answers: what and where you studied, whether you studied as a domestic or international student, and, depending on which form you complete, your graduation or start year, job roles and salaries. They're linked to your account so you only need to share once.

We never ask for your employer.

What's public and what's private

  • Graduates: your answers (course, university, graduation year, student type, time to first job, roles and salaries) appear as an anonymous card in the database. Your email is never shown.
  • Current and future students: your answers are never shown in the database. We only use them in anonymous totals.

Why we collect it

To build a free, independent picture of what Australian degrees lead to. We use your account to stop duplicate or fake entries and to unlock the database for people who have shared their story. We only email you to sign you in. We never share or sell your email or personal details.

On your device

We don't use cookies or any tracking or advertising tools. The site saves two small things in your browser's local storage: what keeps you signed in (sign-in tokens, not a password) and whether you've seen our notice. Signing out, or clearing your browser's site data, removes them.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address like any website request.

Where it's stored

Accounts and answers are stored with Supabase, our database provider, in its Sydney (Australia) data centre. If you sign in with Google, Google handles that sign-in. Sign-in emails are sent by our email provider.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your answers or your account at any time by contacting us. Deleting your answers removes your card from the database.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the Contact link at the bottom of this page.
