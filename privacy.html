Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Draft for review. This is a plain-English starting point, not legal advice. Have it checked against the Australian Privacy Principles before launch.

What we collect

When you fill in the Share form we collect your email address and your answers: what and where you studied, whether you studied as a domestic or international student, and, depending on which form you complete, your graduation or start year, job roles and salaries.

We never ask for your name or your employer.

What's public and what's private

  • Graduates: your answers (course, university, graduation year, student type, time to first job, roles and salaries) appear as an anonymous card in the database. Your email is never shown. Cards from very small groups are hidden so no one can be identified.
  • Current and future students: your answers are never shown in the database. We only use them in anonymous totals.

Why we collect it

To build a free, independent picture of what Australian degrees lead to. We use your email to stop duplicate or fake entries. We never share or sell your email or personal details.

Where it's stored

Answers are stored in Google Sheets, provided by Google, which may store data outside Australia.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your answers at any time by contacting us. Deleting your answers removes your card from the database.
