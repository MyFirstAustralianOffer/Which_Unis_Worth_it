Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Return on Uni (returnonuni.com.au) is run by Greengrape Solar Pty Ltd, ABN 25 627 026 564, based in Sydney, Australia ("we", "us"). We're committed to handling your personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Your account. You sign in with Google or with a link we email you. We keep your email address, your name and profile picture if you sign in with Google, when you joined, and whether you've shared your story. There are no passwords: we never see or store one.

Your answers. When you fill in the Share form we collect your answers: what and where you studied, whether you studied as a domestic or international student, and, depending on which form you complete, your graduation or start year, job roles and salaries. They're linked to your account so you only need to share once.

Technical information. Like any website, our hosting and database providers automatically record basic technical details when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, and the date and time of requests. We use this only to keep the site running, secure and free of abuse.

Messages you send us. If you contact us, we keep your message and email address so we can reply.

What we never ask for. We never ask for your employer, your exact date of birth, your address, or your student ID. Please don't include them, or anyone else's personal details, in your answers.

What's public and what's private

  • Graduates: your answers (course, university, graduation year, student type, time to first job, roles and salaries) appear as an anonymous card in the database. Your name, email and photo are never shown.
  • Current and future students: your answers are not shown in the database. We only use them in anonymous totals.

Why we collect it

We use your information to:

  • build a free, independent picture of what Australian degrees lead to;
  • show anonymous cards and anonymous totals (like average salaries or time to first job);
  • stop duplicate or fake entries;
  • unlock the database for people who have shared their story;
  • sign you in, run the site and keep it secure;
  • reply when you contact us.

We only email you to sign you in, to reply to you, or to send our newsletter if you've signed up for it. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time.

Anonymous totals and research

We may publish or share anonymous totals and trends, for example in articles, social posts, media stories, or with universities, researchers or partners. These never include your name, email, or any detail that could reasonably identify you. We may also provide universities and other organisations with anonymous insights, and we may charge for this. We never give them your name, email or individual answers.

Who we share it with

We never sell, rent or trade your email or personal details. We only share information with:

  • Service providers who help us run the site, under their own privacy and security terms: Supabase (database and sign-in), and Google (if you sign in with Google).
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.
  • A new owner, if Return on Uni is ever sold or merged, who must keep protecting your information in line with this policy.

Where it's stored

Accounts and answers are stored with Supabase in its Sydney (Australia) data centre. Some of our providers, such as Google and Resend (our email provider), may process data overseas, including in the United States. We use well-known providers with strong security practices.

On your device

We don't use cookies or any tracking or advertising tools. The site saves two small things in your browser's local storage: what keeps you signed in (sign-in tokens, not a password) and whether you've seen our notice. Signing out, or clearing your browser's site data, removes them.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address like any website request.

How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit and stored with access controls. Only our small team can access the database, and only when needed. No system is perfectly secure, but if a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and answers for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal details and remove your card within 30 days. Anonymous totals that already include your answers may stay, because they can't be linked back to you. Technical logs are kept by our providers for a limited time.

Age

Return on Uni is for people aged 16 and over. If you're under 16, please don't create an account. If we learn we've collected information from someone under 16, we'll delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your answers or your account at any time by contacting us. Deleting your answers removes your card from the database. We'll respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm it's really you before making changes.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, contact us first and we'll try to fix it within 30 days. If you're still not satisfied, you can complain to the OAIC at oaic.gov.au or 1300 363 992.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site grows. We'll change the date at the top, and if a change is important we'll show a notice on the site or email you before it applies.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us on returnonuni@gmail.com.
