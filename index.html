About

Every student deserves to know what a degree really leads to.

Choosing a uni is one of the biggest financial decisions most people make, yet the information available is mostly marketing. Which Uni's Worth It? collects outcomes straight from graduates: how long it took them to find work in their field, roughly what they started on, and what actually got them hired.

We're independent and not affiliated with any university. Results are shown only in groups, and salaries only as bands, so no one can be identified.
